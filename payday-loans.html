Explore apps and websites that have been associated with short-term or small-ticket loans. Products, availability, eligibility, fees and repayment periods can change. Use the links to verify current details directly before applying.

Important: This is an independent research directory, not a recommendation or confirmation that every app is a lender or is currently authorised by the RBI. Some services are loan platforms or brands rather than the regulated lender. Confirm the actual lender’s legal name, RBI status where applicable, Key Fact Statement (KFS), APR, all fees, late-payment charges and repayment date before accepting a loan. Never pay an upfront fee to an unknown person promising loan approval.

How to check a loan offer

1. Identify the actual regulated bank or NBFC named in the loan agreement, not only the app brand.

2. Read the Key Fact Statement and compare APR, processing fees, net disbursal, total repayment and late fees.

3. Check the lender against current RBI information and use only the lender’s verified website or app listing.

4. Do not treat a search result, directory entry or app listing as an endorsement or guarantee of legitimacy.
